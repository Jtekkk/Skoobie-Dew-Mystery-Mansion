# 🔦 Skoobie-Dew & the Mystery Mansion

A spooky, self-contained **point-and-click mystery adventure** that runs entirely
in your browser — no build step, no dependencies, no install. Explore a 22-room
haunted manor, snoop out clues, solve puzzles, dodge **death traps**, crack
optional **side mysteries**, and unmask the Phantom before your Scooby Snacks run out!

![play in browser](https://img.shields.io/badge/play-in_browser-7bf1a8) ![no build](https://img.shields.io/badge/build-none-b388ff) ![offline](https://img.shields.io/badge/works-offline-ffd166)

## ▶️ Play

`index.html` is a **single, fully self-contained file** — all the CSS and
JavaScript are inlined, so there's nothing else to download. Just **double-click
`index.html`** (or drop it anywhere — a USB stick, an email, a preview pane) and
play. No server, no build, no internet required.

## 🕵️ The Game

- **Immersive scene art** — every room is drawn as a layered, animated **SVG
  scene** (themed lighting, silhouetted props, drifting fog, dust motes, cobwebs,
  flickering candlelight), generated in-browser so it ships with the game and
  works fully offline. Respects `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- **A whole cast of sprites** — Skoobie the detective pup stands in every scene
  and **walks in / sniffs around** as you move and search; the **Phantom** swoops
  in on a scare; and the rest of **Mystery Inc.** (Shaggy, Velma, Fred, Daphne)
  cameo in their favorite rooms and line up to cheer your victory.
- **A roaming Phantom** — the Phantom now *stalks you room to room* with real
  pathfinding. Track it on the **🗺️ minimap** and watch the **👻 proximity sense**
  in the HUD. Get cornered and it'll cost you a Scooby Snack (or your courage).
- **22 rooms** to explore — including **2 secret rooms** you have to *reveal*
  (the Phantom's Lair behind a piano puzzle, and a Hidden Laboratory behind a
  crooked portrait). Some doors are **locked** and need the right key.
- **6 clue types** (cloak, tool, motive, tell, lair, trinket). Four *key leads*
  unmask the Phantom; two **bonus clues** hide in the secret rooms for an
  airtight case.
- **Collect & use items** — a lantern to light the dark, a crowbar to pry open
  the catacombs, rope to cross a chasm, a gas mask to survive the spores, keys,
  a magnet, a music sheet, and more. Your **inventory** is always on screen.
- **4 side puzzles**: a four-digit **safe**, a **piano** lullaby, a **music box**,
  and a **constellation dial** — each with in-world clues and a hint.
- **3 optional side mysteries**: *The Shattered Locket*, *The Dew Family Fortune*
  (a torn treasure map), and *The Phantom's Hideout*. Solve them for rewards and
  a richer ending.
- ☠️ **Death traps.** A bottomless chasm, a poisoned chalice, and a room full of
  toxic spores can end your case for good. Read the warnings — and bring the right
  gear!
- 🦴 **Survival**: the Phantom randomly leaps out to scare you. Scooby Snacks keep
  your nerve; run out and your **courage** drains. Stock up in the pantry.
- 💡 **Hint system** ("Ask Scooby") gives context-aware nudges toward your next lead.
- 🔊 **Sound FX** are fully synthesized with the Web Audio API (no audio files,
  works offline). Toggle them with the speaker button.

## 🎲 Replayable by design

The Phantom is chosen **at random every game**, and the clues are generated to
point at *that* suspect — so the deduction is real, not scripted. Each of the four
suspects has a unique cloak, tool, motive, tell, lair, and trinket, so every clue
narrows the field.

## 🗂️ Project layout

The playable `index.html` at the repo root is a **built artifact** — generated by
inlining the modular sources under `src/` so the game is one portable file.

```
index.html           # ← play this (self-contained build; all CSS/JS inlined)
build.mjs            # inlines src/ into index.html  →  node build.mjs
src/
  index.html         # markup template (links css/ + js/)
  css/style.css      # spooky atmospheric theme + animations
  js/sfx.js          # Web Audio sound effects (synthesized, no files)
  js/scenes.js       # layered SVG scene art (palettes + props + atmosphere)
  js/sprites.js      # character sprites (hero, Phantom, suspects, the gang)
  js/minimap.js      # schematic map + roaming-Phantom AI (BFS pathfinding)
  js/data.js         # rooms, suspects, items, clues, puzzles, quests (content)
  js/game.js         # engine: state, rendering, save/load, puzzles, deduction
```

### Developing

Edit the files under `src/` (you can open `src/index.html` directly to test),
then rebuild the single-file `index.html`:

```bash
node build.mjs
```

Progress auto-saves to `localStorage`, so you can close the tab and **Continue Case**
later (death traps are permanent for that run, of course!).
