How to Play

  • Explore the mansion by moving between rooms with the exit buttons.
  • Search each room to uncover hidden clues. Every clue narrows down the suspects.
  • Munch Scooby Snacks to keep your courage up — the Phantom is prowling, and a good scare costs you a snack!
  • Once you've gathered enough evidence, open the Case File and make your accusation.
  • Pin every clue on the right suspect to win and unmask the Phantom of Dew Manor.
