How to Play

  • Explore the 22-room mansion through the exit doors. Some are locked or secret.
  • Search rooms for clues and items. Four key leads unmask the Phantom; two bonus clues hide in secret rooms.
  • Collect & use items — keys open doors, a lantern lights the dark, rope crosses a chasm, a gas mask survives the spores.
  • Solve puzzles (a safe, a piano, a music box, the stars) to open up optional side mysteries.
  • Beware death traps. A wrong step can end the case for good — read the warnings!
  • Munch Scooby Snacks to survive the Phantom's scares. Stuck? Hit 💡 Hint.
  • When you've got the leads, open the Case File and accuse.
